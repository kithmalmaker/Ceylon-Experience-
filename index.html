Ayubowan & Welcome

The Story Behind Ceylon Experience

I was born and raised right here in Kalutara — a coastal town where the golden river meets the deep blue ocean. For over 10 years, I have had the privilege of welcoming travelers from across the globe (UK, Russia, France, Germany, and beyond) and sharing with them the authentic beauty of Sri Lanka — from serene tropical beaches to ancient misty mountain kingdoms.

I created Ceylon Experience with one simple goal: to provide every guest with a safe, joyful, and stress-free journey. Whether it is a breezy Tuk-Tuk ride through village backroads or a multi-day island round tour in my comfortable A/C van to Sigiriya, Kandy, Ella, and Yala — I treat every traveler like family.

With fluent multilingual communication and local knowledge, I ensure seamless planning and care from your first message to your final airport drop-off. This is my passion and my home.

Curated Experiences

Tour Packages Across Sri Lanka

All itineraries can be 100% customized to your schedule, preferences, and pace.

Half / Full Day Tuk-Tuk or Van

Kalutara Local Heritage & Coastal Secrets

Explore the world-famous hollow Kalutara Bodhiya Stupa, historic Richmond Castle, scenic Calido Beach lagoon, a peaceful Kalu Ganga river cruise, and local fruit & spice markets.

  • Kalutara Bodhiya (World's only hollow Buddhist stupa)
  • Majestic Richmond Castle & garden estate
  • Calido Beach where the river meets the ocean
  • Authentic tropical fruit & king coconut tasting
Full Day Tuk-Tuk or Van

Bentota & Madu River Mangrove Eco-Safari

Cruise through the mystical mangrove tunnels of Madu Ganga, visit Cinnamon Island, receive natural fish therapy, meet baby sea turtles at Kosgoda, and relax at Bentota Beach.

  • Boat safari through 64 mangrove islands on Madu River
  • Traditional cinnamon harvesting demonstration
  • Kosgoda Sea Turtle Conservation & Hatchery
  • Bentota Golden Beach & water activities
Full Day Comfortable A/C Van

Galle Dutch Fort & Southern Coastal Wonders

Discover the 17th-century UNESCO Galle Fort, feed wild sea turtles in Hikkaduwa, photograph traditional Koggala stilt fishermen, and visit a Moonstone gemstone mine.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Galle Fort walking tour & lighthouse
  • Swim with and feed wild giant sea turtles at Hikkaduwa
  • Iconic stilt fishermen photography at Koggala
  • Unawatuna Japanese Peace Pagoda & jungle beach
Full Day Comfortable A/C Van

Mirissa Blue Whale Safari & Coconut Tree Hill

Embark on an early morning boat safari in the Indian Ocean to spot blue whales and dolphins, followed by lunch at Mirissa Beach and photos at Coconut Tree Hill.

  • Ethical Blue Whale & Spinner Dolphin watching boat tour
  • Famous Coconut Tree Hill panoramic viewpoint
  • Mirissa Beach seaside seafood lunch & chilling
  • Scenic coastal drive back with sunset stops
Full Day A/C Van + 4x4 Jeep

Udawalawe & Yala Wild Elephant Safari

Experience a private 4x4 open-top jeep safari inside Udawalawe or Yala National Park. See large herds of wild Asian elephants, leopards, water buffaloes, and exotic birds.

  • Guaranteed wild elephant sightings in natural habitat
  • Private open-top 4x4 Safari Jeep with expert tracker
  • Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home (feeding time)
  • Comfortable door-to-door A/C van transfer
1 or 2 Days Comfortable A/C Van

Sigiriya Rock Fortress & Sacred Kandy Tour

Journey to Sri Lanka's cultural heart! Climb the ancient 5th-century Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress, visit Dambulla Golden Cave Temple, and explore Kandy's Sacred Temple of the Tooth.

  • UNESCO Sigiriya Lion Rock ancient palace citadel
  • Dambulla Golden Cave Temple & giant Buddha statue
  • Kandy Sacred Temple of the Tooth & cultural dance show
  • Spice garden visit and scenic highland drive
1 to 3 Days Comfortable A/C Van

Ella, Nuwara Eliya & Scenic Tea Country

Travel into the cool misty mountains! Walk across the famous Nine Arch Bridge in Ella, climb Little Adam's Peak, visit lush tea plantations & factories, and see Ravana Waterfall.

  • Iconic Nine Arch Demodara Bridge & scenic train route
  • Nuwara Eliya 'Little England' & British colonial architecture
  • Ceylon Tea factory tour & fresh tea tasting session
  • Ravana Falls & breathtaking mountain viewpoint stops
