The Story Behind Ceylon Experience

I was born and raised right here in Kalutara — a town where the golden river meets the deep blue ocean. For over 10 years, I have been welcoming visitors from across the world and sharing with them the real Sri Lanka — not just the famous landmarks, but the hidden beaches, the roadside king coconut stalls, the fishermen's villages, and the warmth of our people.

I created Ceylon Experience with one simple goal: to give every traveler a genuine, joyful, and stress-free journey. Whether it is a breezy Tuk-Tuk ride through Kalutara's coconut-lined back roads or a full-day adventure across the South Coast in my comfortable A/C van — I treat every guest like a friend, not a fare.

Fluent in both English and Russian, I make sure that every guest — whether from London, Moscow, Berlin, or Sydney — feels completely comfortable, heard, and taken care of from the first message to the final drop-off. This is not just a job for me. It is my passion.

Curated Experiences

Popular South Coast & Day Tours

All itineraries can be 100% customized to your schedule, preferences, and pace.

Half / Full Day Tuk-Tuk or Van

Kalutara Local Heritage & Hidden Secrets

Explore the famous Kalutara Bodhiya Stupa, historic Richmond Castle, scenic Calido Beach lagoon, a peaceful Kalu Ganga boat cruise, and local fruit/spice markets.

  • Kalutara Bodhiya (World's only hollow Buddhist stupa)
  • Majestic Richmond Castle & garden estate
  • Calido Beach where the river meets the ocean
  • Authentic tropical fruit & king coconut tasting
Full Day Tuk-Tuk or Van

Bentota & Madu River Mangrove Eco-Safari

Cruise through the mystical mangrove tunnels of Madu Ganga, visit Cinnamon Island, receive fish therapy, meet baby sea turtles at Kosgoda, and relax at Bentota Beach.

  • Boat safari through 64 mangrove islands on Madu River
  • Traditional cinnamon harvesting demonstration
  • Kosgoda Sea Turtle Conservation & Hatchery
  • Bentota Golden Beach & optional water sports
Full Day Comfortable A/C Van

Galle Dutch Fort & Southern Coastal Wonders

Discover the 17th-century UNESCO Galle Fort, feed wild sea turtles in Hikkaduwa, photograph traditional Koggala stilt fishermen, and visit a Moonstone gemstone mine.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Galle Fort walking tour & lighthouse
  • Swim with and feed wild giant sea turtles at Hikkaduwa
  • Iconic stilt fishermen photography at Koggala
  • Unawatuna Japanese Peace Pagoda & jungle beach
Full Day Comfortable A/C Van

Mirissa Blue Whale Safari & Coconut Tree Hill

Embark on an early morning boat safari in the Indian Ocean to spot blue whales and dolphins, followed by lunch at Mirissa Beach and photos at Coconut Tree Hill.

  • Ethical Blue Whale & Spinner Dolphin watching boat tour
  • Famous Coconut Tree Hill panoramic viewpoint
  • Mirissa Beach seaside seafood lunch & chilling
  • Scenic coastal drive back with sunset stops
Full Day A/C Van + 4x4 Jeep

Udawalawe Wild Elephant National Park Safari

Experience a private 4x4 open-top jeep safari inside Udawalawe National Park. See large herds of wild Asian elephants, water buffaloes, crocodiles, and exotic birds.

  • Guaranteed wild elephant sightings in natural habitat
  • Private open-top 4x4 Safari Jeep with expert tracker
  • Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home (feeding time)
  • Comfortable door-to-door A/C van transfer from Kalutara
